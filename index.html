Lifestyle creep and the 12-month rule
  • The 12-month rule: when income rises, keep spending flat for a year and save the difference.
  • The residency-to-attending jump is the one that matters most.
  • Fund big known costs (leave, a car, a wedding) from a separate account, not the brokerage.
  • Size fixed costs to base pay. If the house only works with the bonus, it's too much house.
